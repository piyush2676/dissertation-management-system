What usually causes this

  • The record already moved on

    Someone acted on it in another tab or another session — a topic that was approved while you were reading it cannot be approved twice.

  • The guide is at capacity

    A supervisor can only take as many students as the department allows. Once the last seat is accepted, further requests are refused.

  • The step is out of order

    Allocation follows topic approval; a viva follows submission. Skipping a stage is refused rather than silently allowed.
