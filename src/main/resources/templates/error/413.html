How to make it smaller

  • Compress the images

    Scanned pages and screenshots are usually most of the size. Export the PDF again at a lower image quality, or resize the pictures before inserting them.

  • Export, do not scan

    A PDF saved straight from Word or LaTeX is far smaller than a scan of a printout.

  • Still too big?

    Ask the project coordinator. Large appendices such as datasets can be submitted separately.
